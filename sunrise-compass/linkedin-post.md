A friend asked me a simple question last week:

"Where is the sun going to shine best today, from exactly where I'm standing?"

They wanted to know which window would get the morning light, when the patio would finally be in the sun, and where to stand for tonight's sunset. Weather apps give you a sunrise *time*. None of them show you *where* to look.

So I built one. ☀️

Sunrise Compass turns your phone into a window onto the sun's path. Hold it up, turn around, and it shows:

🌅 Exactly where the sun will rise and set from your spot, with the time
🧭 When the sun will shine from whatever direction you're facing
⛰️ Your real horizon (hills and mountains), so sunrise times account for the terrain around you
☁️ Live local weather, plus a forecast of how colorful the sunrise and sunset should be
🗺️ A satellite map with beams showing where sunrise and sunset land on your street
✨ The real stars, constellations and moon at night

It's a web app, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Open it on any phone, tap Begin, and allow location and compass access. You can add it to your home screen in one tap.

Try it free 👉 https://sunrise.ciprari.ai

The most fun part was learning how much math is hiding in "where's the sun?": solar position, atmospheric scattering for the sky colors, terrain elevation for the true horizon, and taming phone compass sensors (iPhones and Androids report orientation very differently!).

Photographers, gardeners, house hunters, runners, coffee-on-the-porch people: I'd love to hear how you use it. And thanks to my friend for the question. Most good projects start with someone asking "is there an app for that?" 🙌
